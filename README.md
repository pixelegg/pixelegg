# pixelegg Informatik & Design GmbH

TYPO3 agency and IT systems house from Viernheim, Germany. We build and maintain websites for companies, schools and municipalities, since 1995. TYPO3 has been our main platform since 1998, with more than 400 projects so far.

Our view: a website does not need a new design every few years. It needs a system that editors can work with and that stays maintainable.

### What we do

- TYPO3 development, upgrades and long-term maintenance
- Custom TYPO3 extensions
- Hosting and operation of TYPO3 projects
- Accessibility (WCAG, BFSG), SEO and visibility in AI search
- AI solutions for TYPO3 editorial work

### Open-source TYPO3 extensions

| Extension | What it does | TYPO3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [pxe-counter](https://github.com/pixelegg/pxe-counter) | Animated number counter content element. The final value is always in the HTML, numbers are formatted for the site language, reduced motion is respected. | 13.4 LTS, 14 |
| [pxe-compareslider](https://github.com/pixelegg/pxe-compareslider) | Before/after image comparison slider. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard, renders responsive images, no external libraries. | 13.4 LTS, 14 |
| [pxe-composer-report](https://github.com/pixelegg/pxe-composer-report) | Backend report of outdated Composer packages and security advisories, with scheduler task, CLI command and email report. | 13.4 LTS, 14 |

The extensions come out of our own project work. They are licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later and tested against TYPO3 13.4 LTS and 14 on every change. Issues and pull requests are welcome.
